Gameplay

How do I move and shoot?

Touch and drag anywhere on the screen. A virtual joystick appears under your finger. Drag in any direction to move and let go to stop.

You don't need to aim. Your weapon fires on its own at the nearest infected it has a clear shot at, so you can focus on moving and dodging.

What do Dash and Frag do?

Both buttons are in the bottom-right corner.

  • DASH is a quick combat roll. You can't be hurt while rolling, so use it to slip through a closing gap. It recharges in 3 seconds.
  • FRAG throws a grenade into the thickest part of the horde. It recharges in 9 seconds, and its damage grows with your weapon.

Shooting a red barrel blows up everything around it, including you if you stand too close.

How do I clear a level and earn stars?

Reach the level's kill goal and a boss wave appears. Defeat the bosses to clear the level and unlock the next one. Each level needs more kills and its infected hit harder.

  • ★★★ No damage taken and at least 90% of the drops collected
  • ★★ At least 90% of the drops collected
  • ★ The level is cleared
How do upgrades work?

Field upgrades last for the current run. Every 90 seconds and at kill milestones you choose one of three. They come in four rarities: common, rare, epic and legendary.

Permanent upgrades apply to every future run. You get one for each level you clear, and more from supply chests in the SHOP.

How do I unlock and upgrade weapons?

You start with the Shotgun. The Semi-Auto Rifle unlocks after you clear level 5 and the Automatic Rifle after level 10. Pick and upgrade weapons on the SURVIVOR screen. Every upgrade costs scrap and adds damage and fire rate.

What are scrap and gems?

Scrap drops from the infected and from kill streaks. Spend it on weapon upgrades.

Gems are rarer. Monsters drop them, the daily supply drop gives you some, and you can buy them. Spend gems on supply chests in the SHOP, which hold permanent upgrades.

How does the daily supply drop work?

Open the game once a day to claim gems and scrap. The reward grows each day for seven days in a row and then stays at the day-7 amount. If you miss a day, the streak starts again from day 1.

Who is on the radio, and can I turn it off?

That's General Grubb. He teaches you the controls, comments on the fight and tells the story of the outbreak as you clear new streets. If you'd rather play without him, turn off RADIO BRIEFINGS in Settings.

Progress and iCloud

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically. Your levels, stars, best scores, scrap, gems, weapon upgrades and permanent upgrades are saved on your device. If you close the game in the middle of a fight, the same run continues when you open it again.

How do I play on another iPhone or iPad?

Progress syncs through iCloud. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices and keep iCloud turned on, and your progress will appear on the other device. Syncing needs an internet connection and can take a moment after you open the game.

I reinstalled the game or got a new phone. Where's my progress?

If iCloud was on, sign in with the same Apple Account, open the game while online and give it a moment to download your progress. If iCloud was off, your progress was stored only on the old device and was removed together with the app.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account or sign-in is needed, and the game plays fully offline. A connection is only needed for iCloud sync and for in-app purchases.

Purchases

What can I buy?

Last Stand: Outbreak is free to download and play. Everything for sale is optional:

  • Gem packs of 100, 500, 1,000 or 10,000 gems
  • Starter Pack of 200 gems and 200 scrap, which you can buy once
  • Extra Life, which brings you back with full health when a run ends

Prices are shown in the game in your local currency.

I paid but didn't get my gems.

Make sure you're online and restart the game. If the gems still haven't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and the order ID from Apple's receipt email, and we'll help.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds, so we can't issue refunds ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I restore my purchases?

Gems, the Starter Pack and Extra Lives are used up once you buy them, so there's nothing for the App Store to restore. Gems you've received are part of your progress and sync with iCloud, so you'll keep them on your other devices and after a reinstall.

Device and settings

The game drains my battery or my device gets warm.

Turn off HIGH QUALITY 3D in Settings. It switches off real-time shadows, bloom and anti-aliasing, which makes the game much lighter on older devices and your battery.

Can I turn off the music, sound or vibration?

Yes. Settings has separate switches for MUSIC, SOUND EFFECTS and HAPTICS, and ENEMY HP BARS shows or hides the health bars above the infected.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
