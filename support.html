Last Stand: Outbreak - Support

Need help with Last Stand: Outbreak or have questions about surviving the zombie apocalypse? You're in the right place. Below you'll find answers to frequently asked questions and ways to contact us for further assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • 1. How do I move my character?
    Touch and drag anywhere on the screen. A virtual joystick appears at your touch point — drag in any direction to move. Release to stop.
  • 2. How does shooting work?
    Your weapon fires automatically at the nearest enemy within range — no manual aiming required. Focus on positioning and dodging while the auto-fire handles the shooting.
  • 3. How do I unlock new weapons?
    You start with the Shotgun. The Semi-Auto Rifle unlocks after clearing Level 5, and the Automatic Rifle unlocks after clearing Level 10. Each weapon can be upgraded in the Character screen using Scrap.
  • 4. What are boosts and how do I get them?
    Boosts are temporary in-run power-ups that enhance your damage, defense, or utility. You're offered a choice of three boosts every 90 seconds of survival or at kill milestones. Permanent boosts can also be earned by clearing boss waves or purchased in the Shop using Gems.
  • 5. What is Scrap and what are Gems?
    Scrap is dropped by enemies and is used to upgrade your weapons in the Character screen. Gems are a rarer currency used in the Shop to purchase permanent upgrade packs.
  • 6. Is my progress saved?
    Yes. Your best scores, weapon upgrades, permanent boosts, and level completions are automatically saved to your device. Progress also syncs across your devices via iCloud. In-progress runs are saved too — closing the app mid-fight and reopening it will resume the exact run where you left off.
  • 7. Does the game require an internet connection?
    No. Last Stand: Outbreak works completely offline. An internet connection is only used for iCloud sync of your progress and scores across devices, which happens automatically in the background when available.
  • 8. What are the system requirements?
    Last Stand: Outbreak requires iOS 17 or later and is compatible with iPhone.
