A story over the radio

  • General Barnaby “Tin Knees” Grubb (ret.) keeps you company from his fortified retirement home: grim, gruff and surprisingly funny
  • He teaches you to move, dash, throw frags and pick upgrades, and points at the right button every time
  • Take the city back street by street and earn promotions from Rookie to General

Survive the horde

  • Walkers, runners, brutes, spitters, chargers, splitters and crawlers, and every type hunts you differently
  • Dash through a closing gap or lob a frag into the thickest part of the crowd
  • Hide behind wrecked cars and barriers, and shoot red barrels to blow the horde apart
  • Chain kills into streaks for bonus scrap

Street events

  • Once you've cleared your first street, something new happens every minute or so
  • Supply drops: hold the ring while the crate opens for scrap, gems and a rare-or-better upgrade
  • Rescues: walk survivors to the Bingo Brigade's van before the dead catch up
  • Hotwire: start an abandoned pickup and plow through the horde
  • Blackouts: the lights go out, your torch burns brighter and every kill drops double loot

Take down bosses

  • Every level ends with a boss wave: Alpha Brute, Twin Runners, Acid Behemoth and Grave Herder
  • Later levels send several bosses at once
  • Arrows at the edge of the screen show where a boss is coming from

Build your survivor

  • Pick one of three upgrades as you fight: piercing and burning rounds, faster fire, dodge, extra revives and more
  • Earn a permanent upgrade for every level you clear and open supply chests for more
  • Unlock the Shotgun, Semi-Auto Rifle and Automatic Rifle, and upgrade each one with scrap
Common Rare Epic Legendary

Make the front page

  • After every run, the Sallowmere Gazette prints a front page about it
  • A headline, a photo from the street, your numbers and your loadout
  • Share it with friends or save it to your photos straight from the game

Push deeper

  • Every level demands more kills and hits harder than the last
  • Earn up to three stars per level with a flawless clear
  • Daily supply drops reward you for coming back
Midnight Toxic Dusk Blood Moon Ashfall Deep Freeze

Made for phones

  • Quick sessions and simple one-finger controls
  • Grubb's Radio widget for the Home Screen and Lock Screen: the general's line of the day, your streak and today's supply crate
  • Leave mid-run and your street stays on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Progress and unfinished runs sync across your devices with iCloud
  • High Quality 3D with real-time shadows and bloom, or a lighter mode that saves battery
  • Plays offline, with no account and no ads

Speaks your language

  • The game follows your device's language, or pick one in Settings
  • The radio, the Gazette, the widget, reminders and the shop are all translated
